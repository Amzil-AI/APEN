What happens next

Real flow: inbound call, qualification, then book, transfer or callback summary.

  1. Take the call — 24/7 reception (Vapi or other).
  2. Greet — Say the opening line (voice prompt API).
  3. Get what they said — Speech to text (STT); transcript is sent to the API.
  4. Check intent — Appointment, callback, info, emergency, after-sales, partner, other.
  5. Decide action — Book appointment, transfer, or collect callback summary.
  6. Act — Offer slots and create appointment; transfer to the right number; or save a callback summary.

So: Call → Greet → Speech (transcript) → Intent → Action → Book / Transfer / Callback.
